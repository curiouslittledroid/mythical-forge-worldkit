# Items and Materials

**Purpose:** the objects the world runs on: what they are made of, what they cost, how they are
carried, and what happens when they break. [[the-book]] is the testimony book.

Add a note when an object creates a situation — a thing people fight over, or a thing that decides
whether a journey is possible. A list of equipment is not lore; a thing that is scarce is.
