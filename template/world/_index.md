# The World

**Purpose:** A map of content for the canon. One note per entity, one home per fact. The folders
below are the layout the conventions fix; a world that needs none of them leaves the `_index.md` in
place and writes nothing else.

- [[history/_index|History]] · [[society/_index|Society]] · [[peoples/_index|Peoples]]
- [[places/_index|Places]] (and [[places/regions/_index|Regions]]) · [[factions/_index|Powers]] ·
  [[gods/_index|Gods]]
- [[npcs/_index|People]] · [[relics/_index|Relics]] · [[creatures/_index|Creatures]]
- [[items-materials/_index|Items and materials]] · [[plot-web/_index|Plot Web]]
- [[maps/_index|Maps]] — which plates exist, and what they are for
- [[meta/_index|World Notes]] — the vault's contents, and the world's working documents
- [[flags]] — the effect-only flags the engine reads
