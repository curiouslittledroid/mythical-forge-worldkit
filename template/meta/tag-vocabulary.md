index page. The linter reads the fenced block below (`TG001`/`TG002`); the mechanism is the

## Questions

1. **What are the world's themes?** A tag is a thread a reader can follow across the codex. Name
   them before writing notes, or every note invents its own.
2. **Which tags are structural rather than thematic?** (Peoples, regions and eras are usually
   frontmatter fields, not tags.)
3. **Which earn an index page?** Those go in `meta/site-decisions.md`'s `published_tags` as well.
