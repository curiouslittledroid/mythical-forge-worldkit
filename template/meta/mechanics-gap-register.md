# Mechanics Gap Register

**Summary:** Every mechanic this world needs that the engine cannot express — the M-numbers — with
what it blocks and what the workaround is. The engine's own capability list, and the platform
requests built on it, are the worldkit's `docs/engine-capability-register.md`.

**How to use it:** a note that wants a relationship kind, flag, effect, tactic or content descriptor
the engine does not have **logs it here** and uses the workaround. Never invent one in an `engine`
block: the closed vocabularies stay closed. The worldkit's `docs/conventions.md` section 9.6 has the
rule.
