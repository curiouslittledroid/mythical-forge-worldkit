# Calendar

**Summary:** The world's count of years. The tools read the `chronology` block below for the date
prefix and the present year; nothing else may restate it.

```chronology
date_prefix: REPLACE
present: REPLACE_ME
```

## Questions

1. **What do people call this year, and why is year zero what it is?** The prefix is what appears on
   a page ("REPLACE 650"), so it should be short.
2. **What is the present year?** Everything in the vault that has a date is placed against it.
3. **How do people divide the year?** Seasons, festivals, a working calendar of the world's own.

`REPLACE` the answers. The worldkit's `docs/site.md` section 5 covers how the site writes a date.
