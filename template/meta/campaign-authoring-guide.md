# Campaign Authoring Guide

**Summary:** **A pointer note.** How to design a campaign in this world — the thread pattern, the
Campaign and Overlay blocks, the scenarios, the flags — is the worldkit's
`docs/campaign-authoring-guide.md`, vendored at `tools/docs/campaign-authoring-guide.md`. Cite it as
"the worldkit's `docs/campaign-authoring-guide.md` section N".

This note exists so that `[[campaign-authoring-guide]]` resolves in this vault, and so a session
working on a campaign has one place to look. Do not restate the guide here.

## This world's additions

- The worldkit's `example-world/campaigns/` is a complete worked campaign: a thread card, two
  Modules, an Overlay and six scenarios. Copy its shape.
- `REPLACE` — anything this world does differently (its campaign scope, its naming, where campaigns
  live).
