# What to Expect

**Summary:** The player-facing promise, in the world's own words: what a game here is like, and what
it asks of the table. It is shown on the site's landing page and About page, from the
`what_to_expect` list in `meta/site-decisions.md` (the kit's `meta/conventions/site.md` section 4 covers the
landing page).

## Questions

1. **What do players do in a session?** Trade, explore, argue, survive, investigate?
2. **What is dangerous, and is combat a good idea?** Say plainly whether a fight is usually a
   mistake here.
3. **What is the world's attitude to the characters?** Indifferent, hostile, hungry for them?
4. **What must a player accept?** Scarcity, moral compromise, death without a resurrection, no clean
   endings.

**REPLACE:** seven short lines, player-facing, in the second person.
