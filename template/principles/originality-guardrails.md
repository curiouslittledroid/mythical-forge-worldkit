# Originality Guardrails

**Summary:** What this world is **not**. Borrowing is fine; inheriting someone's protected setting is
not. Write the absences down, and add to them as the world grows.

## Questions

1. **What is the nearest well-known setting?** Name it, then name the three things about this world
   that are *not* that setting.
2. **Which protected creatures, names and mechanisms must never appear?** (The worldkit's own answer
   to this, for its first world, is in that world's own guardrails note; a new world writes its own.)
3. **Which tired genre habits are banned here?** Examples worth considering: an ages-of-the-world
   myth, fading immortal peoples, mining peoples defined by greed, physics-free deserts, markets in
   people.
4. **Where is the line between homage and copy?** A mechanic can be borrowed; a signature creature,
   faction or word cannot.

**REPLACE:** the absences, as a short list.

## The test

For every new idea: **could a reader name the book it came from?** If yes, change it or make it
this world's own. The world's causes have to be its own — everything here traces to this world's
history, not to another setting's.
