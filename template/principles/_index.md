# Principles

**Purpose:** The four notes that decide what this world is, before any lore is written. They are
written as **questions** because a boiled-down version of another world's answers is not a world.

- [[pillars]] — what it is for, what is scarce, who holds power, what players do
- [[tone]] — how it feels, and what content it carries
- [[permanence-rules]] — what can never be restored, and why
- [[originality-guardrails]] — what this world must not be

The vault's contents are in [[meta/_index|World Notes]].

Answer them with the user in the first session. The worldkit's `docs/campaign-authoring-guide.md`
is the authority on **how to run that session**; this folder is the world's own answers.
