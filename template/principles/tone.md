# Tone

**Summary:** How the world feels in play, and what it carries. The **content descriptors** are the
engine's closed vocabulary and are declared in `world.config.yaml` (`site.content_descriptors`); the
sentences here are what they mean in this world.

## Questions

1. **Grim, hopeful, or grim with embers?** Where is the hope, and what does it cost?
2. **Weird or grounded?** Does the world have melancholy strangeness, and how close to the surface?
3. **Who suffers, and who is spared?** The world bible should say what a game here will actually
   contain, so that a table can decide before it starts.
4. **What is the register of the prose?** Dry, archaic, plain, wry?

**REPLACE:** the answers, in a paragraph each.

## What a game here contains

Declared in `world.config.yaml` and shown to readers on the site's About page:

- `REPLACE` (the engine's descriptors, e.g. `VIOLENCE`, `HORROR`, `MATURE_THEMES`)

Then write the sentence the reader needs: what kind of violence, how close the horror sits, and
anything the table should know before session one. The kit's `site.md` section 5 covers
how the site presents it.
