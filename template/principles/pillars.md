# Pillars

**Summary:** Six questions. A world that cannot answer them in a paragraph each is not ready to
write lore. **The answers below are the world's; replace every `REPLACE`.**

## 1. What is this world *for*?

One sentence, as if to a player who has never heard of it. Not the premise of a story — the reason
to play here rather than anywhere else.

**REPLACE:** _e.g. "A world where the magic that held the climate steady is broken, and every
community is one bad season from walking away."_

## 2. What is scarce?

Scarcity is the engine of every plot and the reason factions deal with each other. Name the two or
three things a person cannot get on their own: water, metal, safe passage, forgiveness, years.

## 3. Who holds power, and how did they get it?

Not a list of factions — the *shape* of power. Who can say no to whom, and what happens when they
do? (A world bible needs this before it needs a single city.)
